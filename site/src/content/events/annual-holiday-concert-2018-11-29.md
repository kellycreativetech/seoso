Join us for our annual fun and free Holiday Pops concert at Secrest Auditorium

The concert is free, but you need tickets, locations to get those tickets City Tire, Muskingum County Library System, Secrest Auditorium, SEOSO office, and WHIZ. Children and Students do not need a ticket.

And bring a non-perishable food item to share with those in need at this time of year.
