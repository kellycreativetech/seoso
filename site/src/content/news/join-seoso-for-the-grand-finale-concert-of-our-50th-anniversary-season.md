The Southeastern Ohio Symphony Orchestra (SEOSO) closes its milestone 50th Anniversary Season with an electrifying tribute to two of the most legendary icons in music—Elvis Presley and Prince! In an unforgettable night of symphonic rock, Sons of Gladys, the renowned Columbus-based tribute band, will take the stage alongside SEOSO to deliver the Ultimate EP: 50 Years of the Kings.

## Sons of Gladys: A Tribute to the Kings

More than just an tribute band, Sons of Gladys have mastered the art of bringing both The King of Rock ‘n’ Roll and The Purple One to life in a perfectly balanced 50/50 fusion of genius. With equal parts Presley’s electrifying rockabilly swagger and Prince’s innovative, genre-defying artistry, this band delivers a one-of-a-kind performance that transcends time. From the soulful intensity of “Suspicious Minds” to the funk-infused mastery of “1999,” this show is a full-throttle musical journey through the sounds that defined generations.

## A Historic Night for SEOSO

This concert is more than just music—it marks the grand finale for our beloved Maestra, Dr. Laura Schumann. After 26 years of inspiring performances, groundbreaking collaborations, and unwavering dedication to SEOSO, Dr. Schumann will take the podium one last time, making this an event you truly cannot miss.
