The 2024-2025 Southeastern Ohio Symphony Orchestra 50th Season kicks off October 20th at 7pm! Join us at the Secrest Auditorium for an unforgettable musical experience. Puddles Pity Party, the 7-foot sad clown whose golden voice is comparable to rock legends like Tom Jones and Freddie Mercury, has amassed nearly 900K YouTube subscribers and performed sold out shows all over the globe including The Kennedy Center in D.C., San Francisco’s Palace of Fine Arts, London’s Soho Theatre, and a residency at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas. Get ready to immerse yourself in the beautiful melodies and harmonies brought to life by our talented orchestra as Puddles Pity Party performs with SEOSO we are so very excited for this opportunity.

Neil Gaiman recently featured Puddles as guest performer in his specially curated gala for The Art of Elysium in Los Angeles.In 2022, Puddles performed a duet with the iconic Eric Idle in Homeward Bound:A Grammy Salute to the Songs of Paul Simon filmed for CBS and was a surprise guest on the Season 4finale of ABC’sThe Conners. He also landed the coveted John Lewis Holiday advert in the UK with a cover of “All the Small Things” that he recorded with Postmodern Jukebox. Puddles was a quarter finalist on Season 12 of America’s Got Talent and appeared on the 2020 season of AGT The Champions.

Your season ticket includes the following performances:

October 20th 2024 7pm Secrest Auditorium- SEOSO featuring Puddles Pity Party

December 5th 2024 7pm Secrest Auditorium- Annual Christmas Concert

March 23rd 2025 3:30 pm Brown Chapel, Muskingum University

April 27th 2025 7pm- Season Finale-The Ultimate EP- truly a Royal experience as we celebrate Elvis and Prince featuring SEOSO with the “Sons of Gladys “The Sons of Gladys -an all-star band made up of Columbus’ most talented performers- in one evening of incredible music.

Secure your tickets now and be a part of our milestone 50th season celebration. Don’t miss out on this incredible opportunity to witness world-class performances in person. We can’t wait to see you there!
