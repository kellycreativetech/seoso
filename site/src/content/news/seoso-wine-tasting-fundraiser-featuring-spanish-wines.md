The Southeastern Ohio Symphony Orchestra is having a fundraiser at 630 Main in Zanesville on Thursday, February 27th 2025 starting at 6:00 pm. Wine and Tapas tasting $45.00 dollars a person. They will feature 4 wines with small plate pairing for you to enjoy. Tickets are limited to 60 people, so you need to get your tickets. Come enjoy an evening of great wines and delicious food to benefit a great cause. Dr Laura will be performing through out the event. You can purchase your tickets online through Eventbrite or through the SEOSO Business Office.

If you have any questions, you can contact LeAnne Bader-Mock SEOSO Business Manager 614-582-2009 or leannebadermock@seoso.org
