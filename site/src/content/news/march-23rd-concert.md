Be sure to join the Southeastern Ohio Symphony Orchestra performing favorites through time in concert on March 23, at 3:30 pm in Brown chapel on the campus of Muskingum University!

The program features several favorites from our past 49 seasons as we celebrate our 50th anniversary!

From the thrill and excitement of the movies- Music from 007

From the beauty of musicals: The Greatest Showman

Festive holiday music for summer holidays, always a favorite- the Stars and Stripes Forever march

And of course, a classic, music of Mozart’s g minor symphony, featuring an additional contemporary Maestra Schumann special- the Mozart-Michael Morph. Gotta be here to hear this crazy mash up of Thriller and the Mozart symphony!

And, featuring retiring professor Dr Harsha Abeyaratne, performing with the orchestra the famous and beloved 2nd movement of Rachmaninoff’s 2nd Piano Concerto. This theme was ‘borrowed’ by pop artist Eric Carmen in his popular hit “All by Myself”.

You don’t want to miss Dr. Laura’s final performance on the Brown Chapel stage, on the campus of Muskingum University, March 23, 3:30 pm.
