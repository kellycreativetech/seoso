    body: |-
      - SEOSO Board of Directors members will hold a 3-year term, staggered with the other directors. This governing body will help the symphony stay organized, and we ask that you pitch in as needed; for example, you might take tickets at a concert or represent SEOSO at a community event.
      - This group will make the rules and regulations to steer SEOSO, and we anticipate rewriting the bylaws in late 2026 and early 2027. The Board will have final approval for the vision for future seasons, fiduciary oversight, and oversee hiring of staff.
      - BOD meetings will occur at least quarterly. We would like to offer video meetings to accommodate members who are traveling.
      - We expect BOD members to buy a season ticket (when we’re having ticketed seasons again) and to attend symphony events and fundraisers, whenever possible. (Fundraising and grant writing will be led by the Chief Development Officer.)
